Fast Keyboard Nova v1.15

- Quick Settings mouse window now uses the user's supplied reference image exactly as its visual base.
- Transparent touch zones were added over the reference image; the visual layout itself was not redesigned.
- Fixed mouse control actions: touchpad movement, Close, Drag, left click, both wheel controls, automatic movement, right click and Select.
- Right click now prefers Android context-click/long-click and otherwise uses a long-press gesture.
- Left/right click now ensure the cursor exists before attempting the action.
- The single top-left resize grip remains aligned with the supplied reference.
- Existing v1.14 real Send-button behavior is retained.
